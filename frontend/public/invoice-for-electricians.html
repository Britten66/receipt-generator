ESA permits and your invoice in Ontario

Ontario electricians are required to pull an Electrical Safety Authority permit for most residential and commercial work. The permit fee is a real cost that gets passed to the homeowner. Listing it as its own line item keeps the invoice transparent, gives the client a record for their file, and protects you if an ESA inspection or insurance claim ever references the work. InvoicePrepper lets you name any line item exactly what it is, so "ESA Permit Fee" appears on the PDF exactly as written.

Job types electricians invoice every week

200-amp service upgrades
The most line-item-heavy job in residential electrical. Bill the panel, the meter base, service entrance wire, conduit, ground rod, and permit separately. Homeowners shopping for a second opinion, submitting for a home sale, or filing an insurance claim need this level of detail in writing.
EV charger rough-in and installation
Demand for Level 2 home charger wiring is growing every season. Bill the dedicated 40 or 50 amp circuit as its own line, separate from the charger unit if you are supplying it. Homeowners applying for federal or provincial EV rebates often need an itemized invoice that shows the electrical rough-in cost on its own line to qualify for the rebate amount.
Pot light and fixture installation
Many electricians quote pot lights at a flat installed rate per fixture. Add each fixture or a total count at the flat rate. If the client supplied their own fixtures, bill labour only. Either way, the invoice handles it without any extra steps. Your licence number and contact details appear on every PDF.
AFCI and GFCI breaker replacement
Arc fault and ground fault breakers are now required by electrical code for many circuits in renovations and new builds. When you are replacing or adding these during a panel job, list each breaker with its location or circuit label. The homeowner gets a written record of exactly which circuits are now code-compliant.
Commercial service calls
Time-and-material billing with a truck charge, hourly rate, and a parts list. Set net-15 or net-30 terms using the due date field. Your invoice list shows which commercial clients have paid and which are still outstanding, without needing a spreadsheet or a separate system to track it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I include my master electrician licence number on every invoice?
Yes. Add your licence number or contractor number in the business details section of your account. It appears on every PDF you generate, which commercial clients and property managers often need for their records.
How do I bill a job that runs over multiple days?
Add each day's labour as a separate line item with the hours worked, or total all labour on one line at the end of the job. Set a due date on the invoice and use net-15 or net-30 for larger jobs billed to a contractor or developer.
Is there a limit on how many line items I can add?
No. Add as many line items as the job requires. A large commercial call might have a dozen parts plus labour, a truck charge, and a permit fee. Every line appears on the PDF with quantity, unit price, and line total.
Do I need to download an app or install anything?
No. InvoicePrepper runs in your phone or tablet browser. Safari, Chrome, or any mobile browser works. Sign in with Google in one tap. No download, no lengthy setup, no credit card required to start using it.
