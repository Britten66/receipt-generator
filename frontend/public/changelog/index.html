Changelog
What's new

Recent releases. For the full history of how the product has grown, see our story.

May 19, 2026
Row view alignment and tier cards refreshed
  • Compact row view in the dashboard now keeps the status badge and total amount aligned cleanly between rows. Status words like Draft or Voided no longer push the total column around.
  • Tier comparison table and the in-app upgrade modal have been cleaned up. AI features now show in the Voice AI accent color so it is obvious at a glance what each tier gives you.
  • Pricing comparison now shows what competitor invoicing apps actually charge, so it is clear how Pro and Voice AI compare on value.
May 18, 2026
Mobile dashboard polish
  • Stats, filter pills, and the New Invoice button now sit where they should on a phone. The sidebar no longer overlaps or pins the wrong element to the bottom.
  • Compact row view fits properly on a phone screen. The status badge no longer clips on the right edge.
  • Swipe-to-delete on a row is smooth: the card glides, the red Delete button reveals cleanly, and vertical scrolling never gets confused with a horizontal swipe.
May 16, 2026
Compact row view, sidebar redesign, and quick close
  • Added a compact row view for the invoice list. Tap the toggle in the toolbar to switch between roomy cards and a tight rows view. Your choice saves between sessions.
  • Moved the New Invoice button to the top of the sidebar so it is the first thing you reach.
  • Cleaned up the sidebar footer. Billing, Terms, Trash, and Help are now proper labelled buttons instead of tiny corner icons.
  • Every invoice now shows its date right on the card or row.
  • Tap an invoice to preview it. Click anywhere outside the preview, or press Escape, to close it.
  • Improved dark mode contrast so borders, dividers, and buttons stay easy to see.
  • Fixed invoice dates that could slip back a day depending on your timezone.
  • Fixed PDF preview opening a blank tab on desktop.
May 13, 2026
Billing units, periods, and vendor details on PDFs
  • Each invoice can be set to Qty, Hrs, or Days. The column header, input mode, and totals adapt automatically.
  • Added an optional Billing Period field. Appears in the form, the detail preview, and on the generated PDF when filled in.
  • Due date now appears in the invoice detail preview, not just on the PDF.
  • Vendor contact details (address, phone, email, website) now appear on PDFs beneath your business name.
  • Closing the invoice form without saving auto-saves your work as a draft when a client name or line item is entered.
  • Double-click an invoice card to open it directly in edit mode.
May 3, 2026
Invoice preview, icon status badges, and referral promo
  • See an example: click the button on the landing page preview to open a real watermarked PDF so you know exactly what your clients receive.
  • Status badges now use icons instead of coloured dots.
  • Referral gift icon in the nav and on the Pro plan card. Opens a modal explaining how the promotion works.
  • Notification dot clears the moment you open the bell.
  • Due date prints on the PDF when set on the invoice.
May 2, 2026
Notifications, referrals, due dates, and reminders
  • New notifications bell in the topbar with recent activity, follow-up reminders, and upcoming due dates in one place.
  • Refer a friend: send your code, your friend gets 1 month of Pro free, you get 1 month of Pro free when they create their first invoice.
  • Optional due date on invoices with a quick "Set" checkbox that defaults to 14 days after the issue date.
  • Self-reminders on any invoice: pick a follow-up date and the bell flags it.
