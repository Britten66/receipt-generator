What painters include on their invoices

Labour by the day or hour

Enter your daily rate or hourly rate. Multiple days or crew members can each be their own line item.

Paint and primer

List paint by the gallon with quantity and cost. Primer, undercoat, and topcoat can each have separate line items.

Prep and supplies

Tape, drop sheets, caulking, patching compound, roller covers. Add each as a line item with its cost.

GST and HST

Your tax rate applies automatically to every invoice. The subtotal, tax amount, and total are shown clearly.

PDF to email or print

Download the PDF to hand to the client on site, or email it directly with the Pro plan ($9/mo CAD).

Track what is owed

See unpaid invoices at a glance. Mark paid when the deposit and final payment arrive.
