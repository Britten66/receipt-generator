Every plumbing job type, billed the right way

Drain clearing and rooter calls
Drain jobs are usually flat rate. Bill the rooter service as one line, then add extras as separate items: camera inspection, hydrojetting, or an access panel charge. If you return the next day because roots came back, add that visit as a second invoice or an additional line with a date note.
Water heater replacement
The unit, the labour, the fittings, and the disposal are all different costs and should appear separately on the invoice. Homeowners shopping for a second opinion want to know if they are being charged a fair price for the heater itself. Itemizing protects you and builds trust.
Fixture installation and rough-in
Whether it is a toilet, faucet, or new shutoff valve, list the fixture at your supply or marked-up price and your labour separately. If the homeowner supplied their own fixture, just bill labour and any hardware you added. No limit on how many fixtures appear on one invoice.
Emergency and after-hours calls
Add a flat after-hours surcharge line before or after the service call fee. Weekend and overnight calls bill at a premium and that premium needs to be visible on the invoice so there is no dispute when the homeowner sees the total. A written record protects you if a client disputes the charge weeks later.
Rough-in and new construction
Larger jobs run over multiple days and may have a permit fee. Add permit costs as their own line item. Use the invoice due date field to set net-15 or net-30 terms for a general contractor or property developer. Your invoice list shows which jobs are paid and which are still outstanding.

Frequently asked questions

Can I add a parts markup without showing my cost?
Yes. Enter whatever price you want for each part. If a valve costs you $18 and you bill it at $28, enter $28. The invoice shows the client price only. Your cost and margin stay private.
How do I bill a diagnostic fee separately from repair labour?
Add the service call or diagnostic fee as the first line item at its flat rate. Add repair labour as a second line with hours and your hourly rate. Both appear on the invoice so the homeowner sees what each charge covers.
Can I track which invoices have been paid?
Yes. Your invoice dashboard shows every job with its payment status. Mark an invoice paid when the money comes in. Outstanding invoices stay visible so you always know what is owed.
Do I need to download an app or install anything?
No. InvoicePrepper runs in your phone browser. Safari, Chrome, or any mobile browser works. Sign in with Google in one tap. No download, no account setup form, no credit card required to start.
