Our story
How the product grew

InvoicePrepper started in February 2026 with one goal: make invoicing fast enough that freelancers, contractors, and small businesses actually do it. Not another bloated dashboard pretending to be accounting software. Just the thing you need to send a bill and get paid.

Every release since has been about removing friction between work delivered and money in the bank. Voice and text AI parsing came because nobody wants to type the same client name into the same form for the hundredth time. Mobile rewrites came because a contractor finishing a job on a roof needs to invoice from their phone, not wait until they are home at a laptop.

On the build side: the early wireframes, component scaffolds, baseline CSS, and edge function boilerplate were all generated with AI tools first, then rewritten and tightened by hand where it mattered. AI is how a one-person team ships a real product in months instead of years. The product decisions, the voice, the tradeoffs you feel as a user: those are mine. The releases below are every step taken in public.
